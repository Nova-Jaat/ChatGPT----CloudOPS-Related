# Client Discovery Audit & Qualification Framework

Use this script and questionnaire on your 30-minute discovery calls to uncover bottlenecks, calculate ROI, and position your automation solution as an undeniable no-brainer investment.

---

## 1. The 4-Part Discovery Call Structure

### Part A: Rapport & Agenda Setting (2 Mins)
> *"Thanks for hopping on today, [Name]. The goal of this call is simple: take a look at your current workflow around [lead gen / operations / content], see where your team is spending manual hours, and determine whether our automation system makes economic sense for you. If there’s a fit, we can discuss what an implementation sprint looks like. If not, I’ll point you in the best direction either way. Sound fair?"*

### Part B: Diagnosing the Current Bottlenecks (15 Mins)
Ask these diagnostic questions:
1. *"Walk me through what happens the second a lead comes in / when you need to research a prospect / when content needs to be produced."*
2. *"Who is currently doing that work? How many hours per week do they spend on it?"*
3. *"What software tools are you currently paying for? (CRM, Zapier, Airtable, email sequencers?)"*
4. *"Where in this process do things break, get delayed, or slip through the cracks?"*
5. *"If you could snap your fingers and eliminate one repetitive task from your team's day tomorrow, what would it be?"*

### Part C: The ROI Calculation (5 Mins)
Translate time wasted into cold, hard currency:
```
Manual Hours / Week = [H]
Employee Hourly Cost = [C] (e.g. $25 - $50/hr)
Monthly Labor Waste = [H] * [C] * 4.33 weeks

Example:
15 hours/week manual research * $35/hr * 4.33 = $2,273 / month wasted on copy-paste work.
Over 1 year = $27,279 in wasted payroll.
```
> *"So right now, your team is spending roughly \$2,300 every single month just moving data between spreadsheets. By automating this, that entire cost drops to near zero, and your sales reps get 60 hours back each month to actually close deals."*

### Part D: Closing & Next Steps (8 Mins)
> *"Based on what you've shared, building an automated pipeline for this will pay for itself in under 60 days. I’ll prepare a custom Scope of Work showing the exact workflow architecture, timeline (typically 10 business days), and fixed implementation cost. Can we schedule 15 minutes on Thursday to walk through the blueprint?"*

---

## 2. Red Flags: When to Disqualify a Client
- **No Existing Process**: If they don't have a manual workflow that works, you cannot automate chaos. Automate only what already works manually.
- **Unrealistic Budget**: Prospects expecting enterprise solutions for $200.
- **No Authority**: The person on the call cannot make buying decisions.
